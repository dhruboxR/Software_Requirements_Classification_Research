WORKING RULES
FR: Primary demand is an observable operation, behavior, access rule, workflow, or function.
NFR: Primary demand is a quality attribute, infrastructure/technology constraint, accessibility, usability, portability, or performance quality.
UNCERTAIN: Wording mixes both categories or is too vague to classify defensibly without taxonomy or project context.
Security keywords do not automatically imply NFR; a specified security operation can be FR.
These working rules may differ from the dataset authors' original taxonomy.
